I finished my B.S. in Cognitive Science with a specialization in Human-Computer Interaction at UC San Diego in 2023. The major sat between the CSE department and the cognitive science department, which meant half my transcript is programming and systems courses and the other half is perception, design, and research methods. This post is the full list, term by term, for anyone who wants the details behind my resume.

## Computer Science

The CSE sequence covered programming through operating systems. CSE 8A/8B was the intro pair for students who already had some exposure, moving faster into data structures than the 11-track.

- **CSE 8A/8B: Intro to Programming** - Java fundamentals, then object-oriented design.
- **CSE 12: Basic Data Structures & OO Design** - Lists, stacks, queues, trees, and hash tables, with implementation and analysis.
- **CSE 15L: Software Tools & Techniques Lab** - Git, debugging, testing, and the Unix toolchain.
- **CSE 20 / CSE 21: Discrete Math → Algorithms & Systems Analysis** - Proof techniques, combinatorics, then algorithm analysis.
- **CSE 30: Computer Organization & Systems Programming** - C and memory, down at the hardware boundary.
- **CSE 100: Advanced Data Structures** - Balanced trees, graphs, priority queues.
- **CSE 101: Design & Analysis of Algorithms** - Complexity, greedy/dynamic programming, graph algorithms.
- **CSE 110: Software Engineering** - Team software development with agile process. Our team project was [ZooSeeker](/projects/zooseeker), the San Diego Zoo route planner.
- **CSE 120: Principles of Computer Operating Systems** - Processes, scheduling, concurrency, virtual memory.
- **CSE 134B: Web Client Languages** - Front-end web development.
- **CSE 135: Online Database Analytics Applications** - Building database-backed web applications. This led directly to my [web analytics dashboard](/projects/web-analytics-dashboard).
- **CSE 167: Computer Graphics** - Rendering pipelines and transformation math.
- **CSE 191: Senior Seminar in CSE** - Advanced topics seminar.

## Human-Computer Interaction & Design

The HCI core of the major:

- **COGS 1: Introduction to Cognitive Science** - The field survey that started it all.
- **COGS 13: Field Methods: Cognition in the Wild** - Observing how people actually use technology outside the lab.
- **CSE 170: Interaction Design** - Prototyping and evaluation of interactive systems.
- **COGS 121: HCI Portfolio Design Studio** - Studio course building interactive projects; much of what's on my portfolio started here.
- **COGS 123: Social Computing** - How systems shape group behavior online.
- **COGS 187A: Usability & Information Architecture** - Usability testing and information organization.
- **DSGN 1: Design of Everyday Things** - Built around Don Norman's book. Where I learned to ask why a door needs instructions.

## Neuroscience & Psychology

The "cognitive" in cognitive science:

- **COGS 17: Neurobiology of Cognition**
- **COGS 101A: Sensation and Perception**
- **COGS 101B: Learning, Memory and Attention**
- **COGS 107A: Neuroanatomy and Physiology**
- **PSYC 106: Behavioral Neuroscience**

## Data Science & Research Methods

- **COGS 108: Data Science in Practice** - Python data pipelines on real datasets. My introduction to pandas; [Learning From Health](/projects/learning-from-health) was the course project.
- **COGS 14A: Introduction to Research Methods** - Experimental design and statistics.
- **PSYC 60: Introduction to Statistics**
- **MATH 18: Linear Algebra** - The math that everything ML-related sits on top of.

## Science, Ethics & Breadth

- **BILD 1 / BILD 3: The Cell; Organismic & Evolutionary Biology**
- **COGS 100: Cyborgs Now and in the Future**
- **PHIL 28 / POLI 27: Ethics and Society I & II**
- **PHIL 156: Latinx Philosophy**
- **HIEU 109: Nationalism in Europe**, **HINE 127: History of Modern Turkey**, **HIGL 127: Sport in the Modern World** - History breadth.
- **WCWP 10A/10B: The Writing Course** - Two quarters of college writing.
- **MATH 20B/20C: Calculus for Science & Engineering**
