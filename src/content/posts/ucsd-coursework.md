A detailed archive of my coursework for my B.S. in Cognitive Science (HCI Specialization) at UC San Diego (2020-2023). This list highlights the technical and design foundations I built during my undergraduate studies.

## Computer Science & Programming

These courses provided my technical foundation in software development, data structures, and algorithms.

- **CSE 11: Intro to Computer Science and Object-Oriented Programming: Java** - Fundamental programming concepts including recursion, inheritance, and polymorphism.
- **CSE 12: Basic Data Structures and Object-Oriented Design** - Implementation and analysis of fundamental data structures such as lists, stacks, queues, trees, and hash tables.
- **CSE 30: Computer Organization and Systems Programming** - Systems programming in C and ARM assembly, focusing on memory hierarchy, bit manipulation, and architecture.
- **CSE 110: Software Engineering** - Software development lifecycle using Agile methodologies, focusing on team collaboration and full-stack development.

## Human-Computer Interaction & Design

The core of my major focused on the intersection of human psychology and digital system design.

- **COGS 120: Interaction Design** - Principles of user-centered design, prototyping, and evaluation of interactive systems.
- **COGS 187A: Usability and Information Architecture** - Techniques for organizing information and conducting usability testing to improve user experience.
- **COGS 10: Cognitive Consequences of Technology** - Exploring how digital tools and interfaces shape human cognition and behavior.
- **COGS 102: Cognitive Ethnography** - Qualitative research methods to understand how people use technology in real-world contexts.

## Neuroscience & Cognitive Psychology

These courses provided the theoretical background on human perception, memory, and cognitive processing.

- **COGS 1: Introduction to Cognitive Science**
- **COGS 101A: Sensation and Perception**
- **COGS 101B: Learning, Memory, and Attention**
- **COGS 107A: Neurobiology of Cognition**
